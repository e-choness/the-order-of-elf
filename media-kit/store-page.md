# The Order of E.L.F.
### Elves Lurking Festively — a cosy Christmas stealth immersive sim

Wish lists get smudged every Christmas Eve. That's where you come in.

Slide down the chimney, sneak past parents, pets and one very suspicious robot vacuum, and
find out what the child **really** wants — and whether their list belongs to **Santa** or **Krampus**.

**🎄 What's inside**
- 4 missions: Elf Academy, The Johnson House, Frostbrook Cabin, Whitmore Manor
- Real light-and-shadow stealth, creaky floorboards and nosy neighbours… er, parents
- Elf magic: Shimmer, Morph, Second Sight, Jingle Lure and Christmas Cheer
- Hide in wardrobes, under beds, among the presents — or in a cardboard box
- A gossiping cat, a biscuit-loving dog, and Santa's cookies to "borrow"
- Clues, decoys and a final report: right gift + right ledger = promotion!

**🎮 Controls** — WASD move · Mouse look · Shift sprint · Q/C crouch · Space climb · E interact ·
Left click Christmas Cheer / throw · 1 & 2 spells · Tab journal

Best with headphones 🎧 · Desktop browser with keyboard & mouse

*A remake of the ABK Game Jam Team 7 game. Art & sound by Kenney (CC0). Open source on GitHub.*
